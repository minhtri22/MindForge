# OIR-PPV Research Protocol v1.0

## Status

Draft freeze after adversarial simulation review.

## Hypothesis

A system can discover minimal generative invariants from experience, validate them causally, and preserve compact representations that explain existing observations and predict novel manifestations.

## Scope

OIR-PPV is a research framework for invariant discovery.

It does not claim:

- general causal discovery,
- replacement of foundation models,
- automatic understanding of all domains.

## Core Concepts

### Invariant

A minimal causal principle that maintains value across contexts, interventions and manifestations.

### Generator

A latent mechanism that produces multiple concrete implementations.

### Validation

Required validation layers:

1. Observation
2. Intervention
3. Counterfactual validation
4. Cross-environment validation
5. Novel manifestation prediction
6. Self experiment

## Benchmark Direction

Three benchmark families:

1. Synthetic causal environments
2. Software architecture history
3. Creative/content experiment history

## Research Gate

OIR-PPV v1.0 requires:

- reproducible seeds,
- baseline comparisons,
- real datasets,
- human expert comparison,
- transparent failure reporting.
