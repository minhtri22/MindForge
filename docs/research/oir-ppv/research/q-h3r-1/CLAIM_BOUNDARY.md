# Q-H3R.1 Claim Boundary

Q-H3R.1 is a post-hoc/exploratory mechanism diagnosis. It does not:

- change the H3R v1.1 verdict `FALSIFIED_UNDER_TESTED_CONDITIONS`;
- prove a causal failure mechanism;
- establish a universal law;
- prove OIR-PPV invalid;
- prove transfer and robustness are unrelated in general;
- authorize H4;
- authorize an H3R rerun;
- transform exploratory diagnostics into confirmatory evidence.

The strongest supported statement is narrower: under fresh non-test diagnostics using the same representation families, a substantial part of the clean-utility deficit is recoverable by a nonlinear readout, while observation noise, relevant-Z suppression, and support shift do not explain most of the observed failure. A residual candidate-vs-L0 clean-information/representation gap remains after nonlinear readout.
