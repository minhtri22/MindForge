# OIR-PPV Benchmark v1.0

## Goal

Move from toy simulation to reproducible evaluation.

## Benchmark tracks

### A. Controlled causal world

Synthetic environments with known latent generators.

### B. Scientific discovery

Find compact principles from experiment collections.

### C. Software architecture

Discover architectural invariants from history, decisions and outcomes.

### D. Creator systems

Discover content principles from experiment history.

## Metrics

- Compression ratio
- Transfer score
- Counterfactual degradation
- Novel manifestation prediction
- Human expert agreement

## Gate

No claim beyond simulation until real benchmark evidence exists.
