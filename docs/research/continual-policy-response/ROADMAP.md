# CPRM Roadmap

Status: **FINITE ROADMAP v0.1**

## CPRM-0 — Specification Foundation

Status: **ACTIVE UNTIL ZERO-SCIENCE QA PASS**

Deliverables:

- origin / WHY_CPRM_EXISTS;
- evidence inheritance;
- primary target contract;
- eligible population contract;
- baseline contract;
- falsification gates;
- finite roadmap;
- append-only lineage;
- zero-science specification QA.

No scientific execution.

## CPRM-1 — Fresh Response Support & Geometry Qualification

Purpose:

> establish on a fresh all-eligible-boundary cohort whether the frozen
> continuous response vector and A-relative contrasts have sufficient
> measurement integrity, support and non-degenerate variation for prospective
> modeling.

No learned response predictor is qualified here.

Requires a new preregistered scientific protocol after CPRM-0 PASS.

## CPRM-2 — Continuous Response Predictability

Only after CPRM-1 PASS.

Question:

> can frozen observable pre-boundary state predict primary continuous responses
> / contrasts materially better than the strongest frozen simple baseline on
> seed-grouped fresh validation?

No controller.

## CPRM-3 — Policy-Conditioned Factorization

Conditional on CPRM-2 qualification.

Test whether explicitly preserving policy-specific B/C structure materially
improves an already qualified continuous-response object.

No rescue-by-default.

## CPRM-4 — Independent Fresh Replication

Freeze the discovery-qualified model, representation, targets, metrics,
baselines and hyperparameters.

Use a new cohort.

Replication failure closes the predictive formulation.

## CPRM-5 — Decision Qualification

Only after CPRM-4 PASS.

Study direct decision utility/value/regret, safety, robustness,
uncertainty/abstention and resource cost.

This is the earliest controller-evaluation milestone.

## Mandatory convergence review

A formal convergence review is required:

- after CPRM-1 STOP/NEGATIVE if target/population support fails;
- after CPRM-2 NEGATIVE;
- after CPRM-4 replication failure;
- before any CPRM-5 controller implementation.

No CPRM-2.1/2.2/2.3 rescue chain may be created merely because a model fails.
