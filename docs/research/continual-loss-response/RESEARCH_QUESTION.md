# CLRM-0 — Research Question

Status: **FROZEN PROGRAM-LEVEL QUESTION**

Primary question:

> Can observable pre-boundary state predict the policy-conditioned
> cross-entropy-loss response surface of the next continual-learning stage —
> current-task acquisition loss and mean prior-task retention loss — materially
> better than frozen simple baselines on seed-grouped sealed validation, with
> acceptable point-prediction calibration, and then reproduce under an
> independently fresh replication cohort?

Scientific object:

```text
observable pre-boundary state X
        ↓
policy a ∈ {A,B,C}
        ↓
two-axis CE-loss response R(X,a)
        ↓
A-relative response contrasts
        ↓
discovery qualification
        ↓
independent replication
        ↓
only then:
downstream decision-governance review
```

The question is predictive, not prescriptive.

No CLRM milestone before independent replication may choose A/B/C online.
