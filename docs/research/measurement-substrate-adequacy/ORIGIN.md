## Why this is not CPRM rescue

MSA does not ask how to make CPRM-2 possible. It asks whether the current
substrate's endpoint-performance measurement regime is scientifically
informative, or whether endpoint saturation itself is the correct property of
the substrate.

MSA explicitly permits `CURRENT_SUBSTRATE_ENDPOINT_SATURATED` as a valid
scientific conclusion rather than a failure to repair.

MSA may not drop terminal accuracy because CPRM-1 found it unqualified, select
a metric by ranking CPRM-1 outcomes, tune difficulty until variance appears,
reuse CPRM-1 seeds to select difficulty, preserve CPRM-2 as an assumed
destination, or train a predictor.
