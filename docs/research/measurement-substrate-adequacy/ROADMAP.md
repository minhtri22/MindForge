# MSA Roadmap

Status: **FINITE ROADMAP v0.1**

## MSA-0 — Specification Foundation

Specification/governance only. No scientific execution.

## MSA-1 — Current-Substrate Endpoint Adequacy Qualification

Use the existing canonical substrate unchanged. Collect terminal accuracy and
terminal cross-entropy loss at the same frozen endpoint. Classify endpoint
adequacy without difficulty manipulation and without a predictor.

## MSA-2 — Conditional Substrate-Structure Qualification

Conditional on explicit MSA-1 transition. Use only a finite preregistered set of
task/substrate constructions whose constructors existed before CPRM. Distinguish
measurement coarseness from substrate/structure saturation without tuning
difficulty until desired variance appears.

## MSA-3 — Independent Fresh Replication

Freeze the discovery-qualified adequacy classification and exact
measurement/substrate contract. Use a fully fresh cohort.

## MSA-4 — Downstream Research Decision

Governance review only: open a separately named future response-modeling
program, move to another substrate for an independent reason, or stop
endpoint-response modeling in this synthetic family.

Predictor fitting remains outside MSA.

Mandatory convergence review follows MSA-1 genuine saturation, MSA-1 STOP,
MSA-2 negative/STOP, MSA-3 replication failure, and precedes any downstream
predictor program.
