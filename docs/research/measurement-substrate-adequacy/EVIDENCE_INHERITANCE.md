# MSA-0 — Evidence Inheritance

Status: **FROZEN SPECIFICATION**

From KCL, within the tested synthetic envelope, MSA may inherit that a
reproducible continual-learning substrate exists and policy/replay/optimizer
interventions can produce measurable causal consequences.

Crucially, the pre-CPRM KCL-1 harness already defined
`evaluate(model, task)` to return both:

```text
accuracy
cross-entropy loss
```

This predates ACO/CPRM and independently justifies terminal cross-entropy loss
as an endpoint measurement candidate.

From CPRM, MSA inherits governance only: CPRM is terminated as formulated,
CPRM-2 is forbidden, the CPRM-1 cohort is spent, three-component rescue is
forbidden, and the next question is measurement/substrate adequacy.

MSA may not inherit claims that terminal accuracy is intrinsically bad,
terminal loss is superior, the current task is too easy, a harder task is
required, or a future predictor will work.

CPRM-1 values are a trigger, not design data. They may not choose metric
thresholds, difficulty settings, sample sizes, or substrate variants.
