# MSA-0 — Falsification Gates

Status: **FROZEN PROGRAM TRANSITION GATES v0.1**

## Gate 0 — zero-science integrity

Require exact branch/parent identity, explicit non-CPRM-rescue scope, no MSA
scientific runner/result/fresh seed manifest, no difficulty mutation, and
predictor/controller/KCL-7 closed.

Failure: `STOP_MSA_SCOPE_INTEGRITY`.

## Gate 1 — current-substrate endpoint adequacy

MSA-1 must prospectively qualify the unchanged substrate using both terminal
accuracy and terminal cross-entropy loss. The future protocol must freeze
reliability, support, saturation and non-degeneracy gates before fresh
execution.

Permitted classifications must include:

```text
ENDPOINT_MEASUREMENT_ADEQUATE
ACCURACY_COARSE_LOSS_INFORMATIVE
CURRENT_SUBSTRATE_ENDPOINT_SATURATED
STOP_INTEGRITY_OR_SUPPORT
```

No classification opens predictor training automatically.

## Gate 2 — conditional substrate-structure qualification

Only after formal MSA-1 transition. Compare only a finite predeclared set of
pre-CPRM substrate/task-family constructions. Failure to find a useful variant
is valid. No adaptive difficulty search.

## Gate 3 — independent replication

Any adequacy classification that materially changes future research direction
must replicate on a new frozen cohort without tuning.

## Gate 4 — downstream governance decision

Only after replication may a review decide whether to open a separately named
future response-modeling program, move to another substrate for an independent
reason, or stop endpoint-response modeling in this synthetic family.

MSA itself does not fit a predictor.

Stop MSA if measurement integrity cannot be established, reproducible
saturation leaves no independently justified substrate question, a finite
substrate comparison fails, continuation becomes adaptive difficulty search,
or metric proliferation becomes post-outcome rescue.
