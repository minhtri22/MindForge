# PPF-L1/L2 Related-Work Research — Personal Context, Event Semantics, and Open-Source Evidence

Status: **RESEARCH COMPLETE / PRE-EXECUTION INPUT**

Date: 2026-09-03

Scope: second research pass for **Personal Pattern Foundation (PPF)** before executing PPF-L1/L2. This pass focuses on prior academic work, public datasets, event/provenance standards, and open-source systems that are directly relevant to:

- **L1 — Define “Recognize Me”**;
- **L2 — Personal Event Foundation**.

This document does **not** authorize implementation. It exists to reduce reinvention and identify which semantics are already well established versus which questions PPF must still prove itself.

---

## 1. Research questions

### L1

What has prior work already demonstrated about recognizing recurring personal context, behavior, routines, relationships, preferences, uncertainty, and changes over time from personal devices?

### L2

What event, observation, provenance, timing, missingness, data-quality, multi-device, and correction semantics can PPF borrow from existing standards and open-source systems without inheriting their complexity?

---

## 2. Executive conclusion

The literature strongly supports the **feasibility premise** behind PPF: smartphones and wearables can expose enough longitudinal digital traces to identify recurring behavioral context and individual structure. Reality Mining demonstrated social patterns, relationships, significant locations, and organizational rhythms from long-running mobile-phone traces; StudentLife demonstrated longitudinal behavioral trends from smartphone sensing; ExtraSensory demonstrated in-the-wild multi-label context recognition from smartphones and smartwatches; mobility-motif research showed recurring daily structures can remain stable over time.

However, prior work also exposes a methodological trap: most systems proceed quickly from raw sensing to features and classifiers, while real-world data collection is incomplete, platform-dependent, delayed, censored, and frequently missing. Digital-phenotyping research has repeatedly shown that missingness can bias derived behavioral features and that missing data may be generated by OS restrictions, sampling design, device state, non-wear, or user behavior.

Therefore PPF-L1/L2 should remain a **semantic proof phase**, not an algorithm phase.

The second major conclusion is that PPF-L2 should not invent basic event semantics from scratch. Several mature standards already establish useful minimal concepts:

- W3C/OGC **SOSA/SSN**: Observation, Sensor, FeatureOfInterest, observed property, `phenomenonTime`, `resultTime`, result quality;
- OGC **SensorThings API**: `phenomenonTime`, `resultTime`, result, quality, valid time, parameters;
- **OpenTelemetry Events**: occurrence Timestamp distinct from ObservedTimestamp;
- **CloudEvents**: event `id`, `source`, `type`, occurrence `time`, extensible metadata; `source + id` can identify duplicate retransmission;
- W3C **PROV**: provenance entities, activities, agents, generation/invalidation lineage.

PPF should borrow these semantics conceptually while keeping its actual representation small and JSON/dataclass-friendly. It should **not** import RDF, OGC servers, CloudEvents brokers, or telemetry frameworks into the foundation.

The third conclusion is that existing open-source sensing frameworks are valuable as **evidence sources and schema references**, not dependencies. AWARE, Beiwe, mindLAMP, RAPIDS, ActivityWatch, and Open mHealth collectively demonstrate practical acquisition, storage, preprocessing, privacy, and schema patterns, but none directly solves PPF's central combination of opportunity-aware personal patterns, abstention, correction/deletion lineage, and platform-neutral observability semantics.

# 3. Academic prior work relevant to L1

## 3.1 Reality Mining — recurring behavior and relationships are observable

Nathan Eagle and Alex Pentland's Reality Mining work collected mobile-phone traces from roughly 100 people over many months and demonstrated recognition of daily social patterns, relationships, socially significant locations, and organizational rhythms.

Why it matters to PPF-L1:

- validates that longitudinal personal-device traces can encode stable individual structure;
- supports `routine`, `relationship-conditioned behavior`, and `significant context` as legitimate semantic categories;
- does **not** prove that every observed recurrence is a preference or causal relation;
- reinforces that PPF should describe association/pattern, not causality.

Sources:

- Eagle, N.; Pentland, A. *Reality Mining: Sensing Complex Social Systems*, Personal and Ubiquitous Computing 10(4), 2006. DOI: 10.1007/s00779-005-0046-3
- MIT Reality Mining dataset: https://realitycommons.media.mit.edu/realitymining.html
- MIT technical report: https://vismod.media.mit.edu/tech-reports/TR-588.pdf

---

## 3.2 Eigenbehaviors — routine has latent recurring structure

Eagle and Pentland later described `eigenbehaviors`, representing daily human behavior using characteristic components and showing predictive structure in individual daily routines.

PPF implication:

- daily behavior can contain compressible recurring structure;
- PPF should remain open to simple statistical representations before assuming complex neural/HDC machinery;
- this work concerns structure and prediction, not PPF's required semantics of opportunity, deletion, user correction, and observability.

Source:

- MIT Media Lab, *Eigenbehaviors: Identifying Structure in Routine*: https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/eigenbehaviors-identifying-structure-in-routine-2/

---

## 3.3 Human mobility motifs — recurring patterns can be simple and stable

Schneider et al. analyzed individual daily mobility networks and found a small set of recurring mobility motifs covering much of observed behavior, with characteristic motifs stable over months.

PPF implication:

- useful personal pattern structure may be far simpler than a generic pattern-intelligence architecture;
- supports the PPF strategy of trying minimal counts/sequence/motif baselines before complex mechanisms;
- also shows the importance of separating population-level motifs from person-specific evidence.

Source:

- Schneider, C.M. et al. *Unravelling daily human mobility motifs*, Journal of the Royal Society Interface 10(84), 2013. DOI: 10.1098/rsif.2013.0246
- Open article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3673164/

A 2026 smartphone-tracking study of older adults similarly found a small set of recurring travel motifs over twelve weeks, providing recent support that longitudinal mobile traces can expose stable recurring mobility structures.

Source:

- Yang, Y. et al. *Identifying the recurring travel patterns among older people: A mobility motif analysis with smartphone track and trace data*, Journal of Transport & Health 46, 2026. DOI: 10.1016/j.jth.2025.102222

---

## 3.4 StudentLife — longitudinal phone sensing can expose behavioral change

StudentLife continuously sensed 48 students over a 10-week term and reported changes in sleep, activity, sociability, stress-related behavioral signals and term lifecycle patterns.

PPF implication:

- supports `change/drift` as a first-class L1 concept;
- demonstrates that patterns can change over longitudinal periods rather than remain static;
- also warns that correlation with external states is not equivalent to causal or personal-preference truth.

Source:

- Wang, R. et al. *StudentLife: assessing mental health, academic performance and behavioral trends of college students using smartphones*, UbiComp 2014.
- Dartmouth page: https://www.cs.dartmouth.edu/~xia/publication/ubicomp14-studentlife/

---

## 3.5 ExtraSensory — context is multi-label and heterogeneous

The ExtraSensory dataset contains more than 300k labeled minutes from 60 users, captured from smartphones and smartwatches in ordinary life, with heterogeneous sensors and a rich multi-label context vocabulary.

PPF implication:

- current context should not be forced into one mutually exclusive label;
- a personal event can participate in multiple concurrent contexts;
- `context` should be compositional enough to represent combinations such as location + social + activity + device state;
- L1 scenario design should include simultaneous contexts and ambiguous context.

Source:

- ExtraSensory Dataset: https://extrasensory.ucsd.edu/

---

## 3.6 Personal sensing — raw data → features → behavioral markers is a useful separation

Mohr, Zhang and Schueller describe a layered personal-sensing model in which raw sensor data are transformed into features, then behavioral markers and higher-level states.

PPF implication:

- strongly supports keeping L2 observations separate from later pattern conclusions;
- confirms that raw/derived distinction should be explicit;
- PPF should stop before clinical-state inference; personal-pattern semantics are a lower and safer layer.

Source:

- Mohr, D.C.; Zhang, M.; Schueller, S.M. *Personal Sensing: Understanding Mental Health Using Ubiquitous Sensors and Machine Learning*, Annual Review of Clinical Psychology 13, 2017. DOI: 10.1146/annurev-clinpsy-032816-044949
- Open manuscript: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6902121/

---

## 3.7 Personal Informatics — user control and reflection matter

Li, Dey and Forlizzi's stage-based model describes preparation, collection, integration, reflection and action, and emphasizes iteration plus an appropriate balance of automation and user control.

PPF implication:

- explicit user correction/deletion is not merely a storage feature; it belongs to the semantic contract of a personal system;
- PPF must not assume machine inference outranks user knowledge;
- downstream `action` must remain outside PPF authority.

Source:

- Li, I.; Dey, A.K.; Forlizzi, J. *A Stage-Based Model of Personal Informatics Systems*, CHI 2010. DOI: 10.1145/1753326.1753409
- Project page: https://personalinformatics.ianli.com/lab/model

---

## 3.8 Context-aware computing — context should be explicit, not hidden in algorithm state

Classic context-aware computing work defines context around information such as location, time, nearby people/devices and activity, and developed abstractions that separated context acquisition from consuming applications.

PPF implication:

- L2 should expose source/context explicitly rather than hide it inside feature vectors;
- PPF's event foundation should normalize acquisition while retaining source provenance;
- platform adapters should eventually remain outside pattern semantics.

Sources:

- Dey, A.K.; Abowd, G.D.; Salber, D. *A Conceptual Framework and a Toolkit for Supporting the Rapid Prototyping of Context-Aware Applications*, Human-Computer Interaction 16, 2001.
- Context Toolkit: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~anind/context.html
- Chen, G.; Kotz, D. *A Survey of Context-Aware Mobile Computing Research*, Dartmouth TR2000-381.

---

## 3.9 Interactive learning — user corrections can materially improve recognition

Research on interactive machine learning for activity recognition found that proactive human correction can improve recognition performance, particularly when users correct erroneous predictions. Skeptical Learning similarly treats both machine and user labels as fallible and introduces explicit handling of contradiction.

PPF implication:

- L1 is correct to make explicit correction a first-class semantic concept;
- future pattern systems should preserve correction lineage rather than simply overwrite state;
- PPF should not design user input as infallible truth, but explicit user correction must be distinguishable from passive evidence.

Sources:

- Tegen, A.; Davidsson, P.; Persson, J.A. *Activity recognition through interactive machine learning in a dynamic sensor setting*, Personal and Ubiquitous Computing. DOI: 10.1007/s00779-020-01414-2
- Zhang, W. *Personal Context Recognition via Skeptical Learning*, IJCAI 2019 Doctoral Consortium. https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2019/930

---

## 3.10 Confidence calibration — accuracy is not enough

Work on confidence-calibrated human-activity recognition shows that high classification accuracy can coexist with overconfident probabilities, and explicitly evaluates calibration error.

PPF implication:

- L1 should not freeze a confidence formula yet;
- if later PPF exposes numeric confidence, calibration must be independently demonstrated;
- semantic states such as `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` and `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE` should exist even before numeric confidence is admitted.

Source:

- Roy, D.; Girdzijauskas, S.; Socolovschi, S. *Confidence-Calibrated Human Activity Recognition*, Sensors 21(19), 2021. DOI: 10.3390/s21196566

# 4. Academic prior work relevant to L2 missingness and observability

## 4.1 Missingness is systemic, not an edge case

A 2021 meta-study of smartphone digital phenotyping distinguished **missingness by design** from **sensor non-collection**, with OS behavior, phone state, disabled sensors, sampling schedules and user behavior all contributing. It notes that missingness can further fall into MCAR/MAR/MNAR mechanisms.

PPF implication:

- `NO OBSERVATION` cannot equal `OBSERVABLE NON-OCCURRENCE`;
- sampling policy/capture expectation is relevant provenance;
- missingness mechanism may itself be informative but cannot automatically be interpreted as behavior.

Source:

- Kiang et al. *Sociodemographic characteristics of missing data in digital phenotyping*, Scientific Reports 11, 2021. DOI: 10.1038/s41598-021-94516-7
- Open article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8322366/

---

## 4.2 Missing data can directly corrupt behavioral features

A retrospective analysis of 1,178 participants using mindLAMP reported sensor coverage degradation associated with engagement and warned that high missingness can create incorrect behavioral features and faulty interpretations.

PPF implication:

- L2 needs explicit coverage/observability semantics before L3/L4 can evaluate patterns;
- derived features should carry provenance to the observation windows that support them;
- future benchmarks should deliberately inject missingness.

Source:

- Currey et al. *Increasing the value of digital phenotyping through reducing missingness*, 2023. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10231441/

---

## 4.3 Current 2026 literature still reports poor missingness/sampling documentation

A 2026 scoping review found that only a small minority of smartphone digital-phenotyping studies quantitatively reported stream-level missingness or explicit sampling/duty cycles.

PPF implication:

- PPF should make `capture policy / expected observability` machine-readable where relevant;
- provenance should be part of the evidence contract rather than left to research notes.

Source:

- *Smartphone-Based Digital Phenotyping Across Health Conditions: Scoping Review*, JMIR 2026. https://www.jmir.org/2026/1/e84146

---

## 4.4 Passive sensing quality degrades without operational awareness

The 2026 LINC framework reports that passive-data quality remains a major challenge and identifies device/app configuration, OS/permissions, user engagement and technical disruptions as causes. It also shows derived mobility features become less reliable as sampling gaps increase.

PPF implication:

- observability metadata must not be optional decoration;
- future PPF experiments need explicit data-quality gates;
- foundation fixtures should include duty-cycle, nonwear/disconnection, OS kill, stale sync and delayed delivery cases.

Source:

- Calvert et al. *LINC: a framework for maintaining high-quality passive data in digital phenotyping studies*, Scientific Reports 16, 2026. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-41435-0

# 5. Standards relevant to L2 event semantics

## 5.1 W3C/OGC SOSA/SSN

SOSA/SSN defines lightweight concepts for sensors and observations, including:

- Sensor / Platform;
- Observation;
- FeatureOfInterest;
- observed property;
- result;
- `phenomenonTime` — when the result applies to the phenomenon;
- `resultTime` — when observation processing completed;
- result quality.

PPF should borrow:

```text
observation != result
source/sensor != subject/feature-of-interest
phenomenon time != processing/result time
quality/procedure can affect interpretation
```

PPF should **not** adopt RDF/OWL as a foundation dependency.

Sources:

- W3C/OGC SSN/SOSA: https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-ssn-2023/
- Earlier W3C recommendation: https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-ssn/

---

## 5.2 OGC SensorThings API

SensorThings formalizes Observation with `phenomenonTime`, `resultTime`, `result`, optional `resultQuality`, `validTime`, and environmental parameters.

PPF relevance:

- provides independent standards support for separating behavior time from result/processing time;
- `resultQuality` suggests L2 should carry observation-quality/coverage metadata separately from behavioral payload;
- PPF should remain far smaller than the SensorThings entity graph.

Source:

- OGC SensorThings API Part 1: Sensing 1.1: https://docs.ogc.org/is/18-088/18-088.html

---

## 5.3 OpenTelemetry Events

OpenTelemetry's event semantic conventions distinguish:

- event Timestamp: when the event occurred;
- ObservedTimestamp: when the collector observed/received it.

PPF relevance:

This maps closely to PPF's proposed:

```text
occurred_at
observed_at / ingested_at
```

PPF should preserve this distinction explicitly.

Source:

- https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/semconv/general/events/

---

## 5.4 CloudEvents

CloudEvents provides a deliberately small event envelope containing required `id`, `source`, `type`, `specversion`, and optional occurrence `time`, subject and data-schema metadata. The specification states that `source + id` uniquely identify a distinct event and may identify duplicate retransmission.

PPF relevance:

Borrow conceptually:

```text
id
source
type
time
schema/version
```

But PPF needs additional personal-evidence semantics absent from CloudEvents:

```text
observability
opportunity
correlation/dedup relationship
user correction/deletion lineage
raw vs derived
```

Source:

- CloudEvents specification: https://github.com/cloudevents/spec/blob/main/cloudevents/spec.md

---

## 5.5 W3C PROV

W3C PROV models provenance through entities, activities and agents, including generation, derivation, influence and invalidation relationships.

PPF relevance:

- strongly supports maintaining lineage for corrected/superseded/deleted observations and derived evidence;
- PPF does not need the full PROV ontology; a few lineage identifiers/relations may be sufficient.

Sources:

- PROV primer: https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-primer/
- PROV namespace: https://www.w3.org/ns/prov

# 7. What existing work does NOT solve for PPF

Despite substantial related work, the following combination remains specific to PPF and must be proven directly:

1. **Opportunity-aware personal evidence**
   - denominator must distinguish real opportunity from simple recurrence count.

2. **Observable non-occurrence vs missingness**
   - not simply sensor missing-data imputation.

3. **Platform capability as evidence state**
   - permission/entitlement/history/background availability affects what can be concluded.

4. **Cross-device evidence relationship**
   - distinguish synchronized copy, same-origin duplicate, independent corroboration, unknown relation.

5. **Correction/deletion lineage**
   - support future invalidation/recomputation without stale resurrection.

6. **Abstention as semantic output**
   - recognition includes the ability to say evidence is insufficient, conflicting, stale or unobservable.

7. **Personal-pattern semantics below clinical inference**
   - PPF should recognize behavior/context, not infer diagnosis or treatment.

8. **Minimality**
   - prove all of the above without importing a large sensing/ontology/ML framework.

# 8. Research-driven changes recommended before L1/L2 execution

The current PPF-L1/L2 protocol is directionally correct, but this research suggests several clarifications should be frozen before execution.

## R1 — Add `observation quality / coverage` to L2 semantics

Reason:

SOSA/SensorThings include result quality; digital-phenotyping literature shows data coverage materially changes downstream feature reliability.

Minimum concept:

```text
quality/coverage metadata
capture policy / expected sampling where relevant
```

This must describe evidence quality, not behavioral confidence.

---

## R2 — Refine time semantics from two timestamps to three conceptual times

Current protocol has `occurred_at` and `ingested_at`.

Research suggests distinguishing conceptually:

```text
phenomenon_time / occurred_at
result_or_observed_at
Ingested_at
```

These may collapse when identical, but the model must represent cases where:

```text
behavior occurred at 08:00
watch derived result at 08:02
phone received/synced at 10:00
```

This follows SOSA/SensorThings and OpenTelemetry independently.

---

## R3 — Add acquisition/capture policy provenance

For intermittent sensing, duty-cycle sampling and history-window limits, the reviewer must know what observation was expected.

Conceptually:

```text
capture_mode
expected_coverage / sampling_policy reference
history_window / access scope
```

Do not put OS-specific fields in the generic event payload; represent them through source/provenance metadata.

---

## R4 — Explicitly allow multi-label current context in L1

ExtraSensory and context-aware computing literature demonstrate overlapping context labels.

L1 scenarios should include:

```text
walking + commuting + with spouse + Friday evening
```

and must not force a single context category.

---

## R5 — Explicitly separate evidence confidence from behavioral-pattern confidence

A source observation may have poor quality while a pattern may still be strongly supported by other observations.

Do not overload one `confidence` field.

Conceptual separation:

```text
observation_quality
pattern_confidence   # only admitted later if calibrated
```

---

## R6 — Add user labels/corrections as provenance-bearing observations

Interactive-learning literature supports treating user feedback as its own evidence source, not silent state mutation.

L2 should be able to represent:

```text
USER_ASSERTION
USER_CORRECTION
USER_REJECTION
USER_DELETE_REQUEST
```

with lineage to affected evidence/pattern state.

This does not mean user labels are algorithmically infallible; that policy belongs later.

---

## R7 — L2 should define a tiny envelope, not an ontology

Recommended conceptual envelope inspired by CloudEvents/SOSA/OpenTelemetry:

```text
identity
source/provenance
type
subject/feature-of-interest
phenomenon/occurrence time
observed/result time
ingestion time
payload/result
quality/coverage
observability/opportunity state
correlation/lineage
schema version
```

The implementation proof should remain JSON fixtures + validator only.

---

# 9. Recommended L1 research fixture additions

Add scenarios that test:

1. multi-label context;
2. high recurrence but no meaningful alternatives — must not become preference;
3. explicit user statement conflicting with passive observations;
4. user correction that later appears inconsistent with new passive evidence;
5. stable routine under one context but not another;
6. uncertainty where system should expose ambiguity rather than collapse to one pattern;
7. source-quality disagreement between phone and watch;
8. pattern remains valid despite one degraded sensor stream;
9. apparent behavior change explained only by telemetry coverage change — must abstain from drift conclusion;
10. relationship-conditioned pattern with identity anonymized/stable internal entity reference.

---

# 10. Recommended L2 fixture additions

Beyond the existing platform fixtures, explicitly add:

1. phenomenon time != result time != ingestion time;
2. sampling by design vs unexpected non-collection;
3. known 10% duty cycle with no event in off-cycle;
4. source quality degraded but still available;
5. two independent sensors corroborating one behavior;
6. one sensor result derived from another sensor result — not independent corroboration;
7. CloudEvents-like duplicate retransmission with identical source + source_event_id;
8. source record corrected after synchronization;
9. user correction referencing an earlier passive event;
10. same semantic event represented by Android and iOS adapters with equivalent normalized output;
11. multi-label context payload;
12. timezone/DST change with stable UTC occurrence ordering;
13. health repository history-window truncation;
14. wearable nonwear distinguished from inactivity;
15. platform upgrade changes source capability without behavioral change.

Recommended L2 fixture floor after revision: **40+**, still without any pattern algorithm.

---

# 11. Candidate related work to carry forward into later PPF layers

These are **not** admitted into L1/L2 implementation, but should remain in the bibliography for L3-L5:

- simple frequency / count / threshold baselines;
- exponential decay and recency weighting;
- context-conditioned counts;
- Markov / sequence models;
- mobility motifs;
- Bayesian-network activity prediction;
- calibrated classifiers;
- interactive/human-in-the-loop correction;
- uncertainty-aware recognition.

No HDC, SLM, vector database, graph engine or LLM mechanism receives protected status from this research.

# 12. Final research decision

## PPF-L1 feasibility premise

**SUPPORTED FOR PROTOCOL EXECUTION**

Prior literature demonstrates that recurring personal context, routines, relationships and longitudinal behavioral changes can be observed from personal-device traces. L1 still must define a precise, implementation-independent semantic contract and abstention behavior.

## PPF-L2 feasibility premise

**SUPPORTED FOR PROTOCOL EXECUTION WITH RESEARCH-INFORMED REVISIONS**

Existing standards and open-source systems strongly support the core event concepts PPF needs. The remaining research contribution is not inventing an event standard, but proving a **minimal personal-evidence extension** that correctly represents:

```text
observability
opportunity
missingness
quality/coverage
multi-device relationship
correction/deletion lineage
```

without platform coupling or architecture creep.

## Implementation authorization

```text
PPF pattern implementation: NOT AUTHORIZED
PPF mobile adapters: NOT AUTHORIZED
MindForge-Mobile integration: NOT AUTHORIZED
```

Next permitted action:

> Revise/freeze PPF-L1/L2 protocol with the research-driven clarifications above, then execute semantic/scenario/fixture proof only.
