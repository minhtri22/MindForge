# ACO Roadmap

Status: **FINITE ROADMAP v0.1**

ACO intentionally avoids an unbounded “one more feature” sequence.

## ACO-0 — Origin audit and program freeze

Status: **CLOSED BY INITIALIZATION COMMIT**

Deliverables:

- origin reconstruction;
- charter;
- evidence inheritance;
- research question;
- falsification gates;
- finite roadmap.

No scientific execution.

## ACO-1 — Target-Stability Qualification

Question:

> Is the historical hard safe-action/mechanism target materially concentrated near its frozen continuous decision margins?

Method:

- fresh cohort;
- matched A/B/C outcomes;
- unchanged KCL thresholds;
- margin and diagnostic label-sensitivity analysis only.

No predictor and no controller.

## ACO-2 — Policy-Specific Continuous Outcome Predictability

Question:

> Are plasticity/retention/accuracy outcomes and A-relative action contrasts prospectively predictable from observable pre-boundary state?

Requires a new frozen protocol after ACO-1 closure.

## ACO-3 — Policy-Conditioned Factorization

Only if ACO-2 PASS.

Question:

> Does preserving B/C policy identity materially improve prediction of qualified continuous quantities?

No controller yet.

## ACO-4 — Independent Replication

Fresh cohort, frozen model/target/features/hyperparameters.

No tuning.

## ACO-5 — Decision Qualification

Only if ACO-4 PASS.

Study policy value/regret, safety, robustness, uncertainty/abstention and resource cost.

This is the earliest milestone at which a controller can be scientifically evaluated.

## Mandatory convergence review

A formal convergence review is required:

- after ACO-2 NEGATIVE;
- after ACO-4 replication failure;
- or before any ACO-5 controller implementation.

No ACO-2.1/2.2/2.3 feature-rescue chain may be created without a new independently justified uncertainty.

## STOP conditions

Stop the active program if:

- fresh support is insufficient under frozen design;
- matched counterfactual integrity cannot be reproduced;
- continuous outcomes fail material baseline-superiority qualification;
- replication fails;
- the research object drifts back into hard-label tuning without new evidence.

Protected KCL confirmatory seeds remain untouched throughout ACO-0..ACO-4.
