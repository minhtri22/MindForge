# Personal Intelligence Teacher (PIT) Research Charter

## Status

INITIALIZED

## Research Question

What type of teacher can provide useful supervision for a Personal Intelligence model?

## Motivation

Track-A demonstrated:

A large general LLM is not automatically a Personal Intelligence Teacher.

PPF demonstrated:

Personal intelligence requires different semantics:

- evidence
- uncertainty
- correction
- drift
- forgetting
- contextual patterns

## Teacher Separation Principle

General Intelligence Teacher != Personal Intelligence Teacher

## General Intelligence Teacher

Examples:

Qwen
GPT
Claude

Purpose:

- language capability
- reasoning
- coding
- world knowledge
- general assistant behavior

## Personal Intelligence Teacher

Research target.

Questions:

- What capabilities are required?
- What model family is appropriate?
- Can an LLM generate useful personal supervision?
- Is a hybrid architecture required?

## Non Goals

PIT will NOT:

- replace PPF
- redesign kernel
- build general LLM
- start training immediately
- select final teacher model yet
